## Interpretation boundary

- Covalent values are the approved BODR/Pyykko single-bond additive radii in angstrom without numerical conversion.
- Van der Waals values are approved Charry Martinez-Tkatchenko Table S1 central free-atom radii converted from bohr using `0.529177210544 angstrom/bohr` and rounded to six decimal places.
- This comparison does not change or reinterpret the existing connectivity multiplier, steric clearance formula, placement parameters, or display scale.

## Approved regression decision

- The original synthetic pyridine fixture used a regular-hexagon side length of 1.0 angstrom. The approved free-atom van der Waals radii moved one unrelated carbon contact from just outside to just inside the existing strict steric cutoff.
- The user approved changing only this synthetic fixture's ring side length to 1.4 angstrom so the test continues to verify deterministic mixed-anchor selection instead of depending on an accidental radius-boundary contact.
- Production placement formulas, thresholds, user-facing placement defaults, and Au electrode resources remain unchanged.

## Full-suite regression gate

The first full offline run after the approved fixture adjustment reported 1,270 passed, 3 failed, and 2 skipped tests. Two distinct effects remain:

- Both packaged Au59 templates have a nearest Au-Au distance of approximately 2.88363 angstrom. With the unchanged connectivity multiplier of 1.10, the previous 1.36-angstrom Au covalent radius admitted 219 template bonds at a 2.992-angstrom cutoff, while the approved 1.24-angstrom radius admits zero bonds at a 2.728-angstrom cutoff. This breaks the protected electrode-template connectivity behavior and requires an explicit decision; the tests must not be changed to accept an edgeless electrode.
- A separate unit-side synthetic pyridine preview fixture crosses the same approved van der Waals steric boundary as the already-adjusted deterministic-selection fixture: its best clearance changes to -0.045483 angstrom against C3. A read-only check confirmed that a 1.4-angstrom synthetic ring side restores a valid preview without changing production formulas, thresholds, or defaults. This second fixture has not been changed pending explicit approval.

### User-approved resolution

On 2026-09-14 the user explicitly approved both remaining actions:

- Use `2.89 angstrom` as a Moltage internal Au-Au connectivity cutoff only for the packaged Au59 templates. This preserves their 219 inferred template bonds without changing the Pyykko table or the general molecular-connectivity multiplier.
- Change the separate synthetic pyridine preview ring side from 1.0 to 1.4 angstrom. This fixture-only adjustment preserves its angle-annotation purpose and does not define a production geometry default.
